Concentric+ for Wear OS

The Pixel Watch's Concentric, on any Wear OS watch

The closest recreation of Google's Concentric face you can put on your wrist. The rings sweep, the 64 colorways are all there, and the complications and always-on look are drawn the way the Pixel draws them. It's built natively in Watch Face Format, the way Google builds its own faces.

Try it here What makes it the closest

Coming soon to Google Play. Free, for Wear OS 5.1 and newer (Half Dial on Wear OS 7). No ads, no accounts, no tracking.

The watch's settings, to try here first

On the watch, long-press Concentric+ and tap Customize. You get these same options. Try them on the watch on this page.

Reading the time

The hour sits in the middle. Around it, the minute ring and the second ring sweep, never ticking, so the current minute and second always line up at the pill on the right. Drag the slider under the watch, press play to run the clock a minute a second, or jump to a time:

Flavors

Porcelain

A colorway and a layout in one tap, like the flavors on the Pixel. Porcelain, Google's default, is where a fresh install starts. Campari and Unicorn use Half Dial, so they need Wear OS 7.

Color

Porcelain

All 64 of the Pixel's colorways, in the same order. Each is four colours with the same jobs as on the Pixel: the time, the rings, the complication tracks and the icons. Complications take on the colorway too, whatever colours the app behind them sends.

Layout

Dials and Arcs

Dials keeps just the rings. Dials and Arcs adds four complications that curve around the corners. Half Dial slides the clock left to make room for three round ones, on Wear OS 7.

Ticks

Full

Google's tick rings, a sparser ring with one tick every five, or none at all for a face of just the numbers.

Always-on

Minutes + seconds ring

As on the Pixel, the rings shrink a little and switch to lighter ticks, the second digits go, and the pill closes into a ring around the minute. Complication text and icons stay, their arcs don't. Three more styles go quieter still.

And the extras

A few things the Pixel's face doesn't have. The left pill is a fifth complication that mirrors the minute pill, and placeholder bars can fill out an arc behind short text. They're all off on a fresh install, so the face starts out looking just like Google's.

The closest replica

Measured against the original, not eyeballed

The idea of Concentric is easy to copy. What makes it feel like the Pixel's is the details, so Concentric+ starts there: Google's own layout, colours and complication rules, carried over one by one.

1:1

Google's geometry

Every ring, digit, tick and pill sits where it sits on the Pixel, worked out from Google's own layout and scaled to the Wear OS canvas. The rings sweep smoothly instead of ticking, as they do on the Pixel.

64

Every colorway

The Pixel's full palette, from Fog to Mariner, in the same order with the same default. Each colour does the same job it does on the Pixel, down to the 50% rings and the grey of an empty track.

°

Complications done right

Ratings get a dot on a full track, temperatures their low and high, progress and goals a fill that brightens past the target. Icons and colour ramps are repainted in the colorway, so no app can clash with the face.

Aa

The right type

Google Sans Flex, tuned so the time matches the widths of the Pixel's clock font to within about 1%. It's openly licensed, so nothing is lifted from Google's apps.

◐

Always-on, eased

On Wear OS 6 and newer the switch to always-on uses Google's own easing: the rings settle 3% smaller and the pill folds into a ring.

+

Plus a little more

A left pill complication, four always-on styles, three tick styles and a few switches. All of them are off until you want them.

Watch Face Format native

No app running on your watch

Concentric+ is written entirely in Watch Face Format, the declarative format Google made for Wear OS faces. There's no code in it at all: the watch's own system draws it.

0

Zero lines of code

Nothing of its own runs in the background, so there's nothing to drain the battery, crash or phone home. It can't collect anything, and it doesn't.

3

One face, three builds

Google Play sends each watch the best build it can run: Wear OS 5.1 gets the face, Wear OS 6 adds the eased always-on transitions, and Wear OS 7 adds Half Dial.

✓

Checked on every change

Every build passes Google's Watch Face Format validator and its memory-footprint check, at the limits Google recommends for Play, before it ships.

Install

Coming soon to Google Play

Concentric+ will be free for watches with Wear OS 5.1 or newer. Once it's out, it's on your wrist in a minute.

1

Get it from Google Play

Open the Play Store on your watch and search for Concentric+, or install it to your watch from the listing on your phone.

2

Phone won't offer it?

Some phones don't list watch faces. The same listing then installs a small Concentric+ phone app. Tap Install on watch and the listing opens on your paired watch.

3

Set it as your face

  1. Long-press your current watch face.
  2. Swipe and tap Add watch face (+).
  3. Pick Concentric+ from the list.
