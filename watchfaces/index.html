Watch faces for Wear OS

Three faces, each built in Watch Face Format, so the watch itself draws them and nothing of mine runs in the background. None of them have ads or accounts, and none of them collect anything.

Concentric+

Watch face · coming soon

The closest recreation of the Pixel Watch's Concentric face, for any Wear OS watch: sweeping minute and second rings, all 64 colorways, three layouts and Pixel-style complications. Built natively in Watch Face Format.

Try it live

WatchSky

Watch face

The real sky behind the time: dawn, golden hour, sunset and stars, with the sun and moon crossing the face and the weather changing the sky. It runs entirely on the watch and collects nothing.

Try it live Google Play

Spiralis

Watch face

A golden spiral is the hour hand. It turns 30° an hour and lands on that hour's line, with four complications, thirteen palettes, 12- or 24-hour numerals (or none), hour lines that fade out or swirl into the center, and a barely-lit always-on mode in four colors. It runs entirely on the watch and collects nothing.

Try it live Google Play

Privacy

The watch faces don't collect, store or send any personal data. There are no accounts, ads or analytics. One privacy policy covers them all.
