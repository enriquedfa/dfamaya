Wear OS

Watch Faces

Simple watch faces for Wear OS: a clear time and room for the complications you pick. They run on the watch and don't collect anything.

Get it on Google Play Support

Customizing

Long-press your watch face and tap Customize (or the gear icon). From there you can change colors, styles and complications. Wear OS keeps your choices on the watch.

Privacy

The watch faces don't collect, store or send any personal data. There are no accounts, ads or analytics. Read the privacy policy.
