Filed for you, on your phone

            
You never have to sort anything. Every piece gets a kind and tags the moment it lands, and a title when it needs one.

Privacy

            
Your library lives on your phone

            
No account, no sync, no ads, and no analytics of its own. The developer never sees what you save.

            
Read the privacy policy

          

          
    
            
  • 
              
              It stays in Kakera’s private storagePictures and files are copied in when you save them, so they keep working after the other app forgets them.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              
              Kakera itself sends one thing online: link previewsSaving a link fetches its title and picture, over HTTPS, with no cookies. Turn previews off and Kakera makes no network requests of its own. (Google’s text-in-pictures reader, if you download it, may send Google diagnostics; the policy has the details.)

  • 
              
              Your backups are your filesBack up to a file you choose, by hand or on a schedule, and restore it on any phone. Android’s cloud backup never copies your library.

Questions

          
Good to know

          
Something else? Email me at developerdfa@gmail.com, or see support.

        

        

          

            Which phones does it work on?
            
Android 8 and newer. The edge sidebar needs Android 12. Smart filing with Gemini Nano works on phones that have Android’s on-device model; every other phone files with Kakera’s own rules.

          

          

            What does Gemini Nano do with my pieces?
            
It reads each piece on your phone and answers with a title, a kind, a few tags and a one-line summary. Nothing is sent anywhere, and it only runs while Kakera is open. You can rethink any piece, or all of them, from the app.

          

          

            Does it work offline?
            
Yes. Only link previews need a connection, plus the downloads you start yourself: Gemini Nano and the text-in-pictures reader.

          

          

            How do I move my library to a new phone?
            
Export a backup file in Settings › Backups and restore it on the new phone, or use Android’s phone-to-phone transfer when you set it up.

          

          

            Why does the sidebar need “Display over other apps”?
            
To draw its drop zone over the app you’re dragging from. Samsung phones call it “Appear on top”. The sidebar is off until you turn it on, and it shows only during a drag.

          

          

            What does “Kakera” mean?
            
欠片, Japanese for a fragment or a shard: a piece of something. The app keeps the pieces.
