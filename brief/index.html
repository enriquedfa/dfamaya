Questions

Good to know

What do I need?

A watch with Wear OS 4 or newer, and an Android phone with Android 11 or newer. Brief doesn't work with iPhone, and the watch app needs the phone app to run.

Which watch faces work?

Any watch face with a complication slot. Brief fits the small circles and rings, but it looks best in a wide slot, where there's room for two lines.

Is it free?

Yes. There's a tip jar in the app if you'd like to say thanks. It doesn't unlock anything.

Can other apps put things on it?

Yes. Turn on Automation in the app's advanced settings, and Tasker (or anything else that can send an intent) can show its own text on your watch.

Something isn't working

Send feedback from the app's settings, or email developerdfa@gmail.com. If you allow it, the app can attach logs from both your phone and your watch. I read every message.

Try it on your watch

Install Brief on your phone first. It walks you through getting the watch app too.
