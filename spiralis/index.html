How it works

One curve, twelve hours

φ

A golden spiral

Every quarter turn, the spiral grows by the golden ratio, φ ≈ 1.618.

r = 5 · φ2θ/π
30°

30° an hour

Each hour line is the spiral itself, frozen where it lies on the hour.

Install

On your wrist in a minute

Free on Google Play, for Wear OS 5.1 and up.

1

Get it from Google Play

Open the Play Store on your watch and search for Spiralis, or use the Google Play button on this page and install it to your watch.

2

Phone won't offer it?

If your phone doesn’t list watch faces, install the small Spiralis phone app and tap Install on watch.

3

Set it as your face

  1. Long-press your current watch face.
  2. Swipe and tap Add watch face (+).
  3. Pick Spiralis from the list.
