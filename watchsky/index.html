WatchSky for Wear OS

A living sky on your wrist

WatchSky paints the real sky behind the time. It follows the sun where you live: it lightens at dawn, turns gold at sunset and fills with stars at night, and dawn and dusk move with the seasons. The sun and moon cross the face on an arc, and the sky changes with the weather.

A sky that keeps time

Six layers of sky blend into each other as the sun climbs and sets: night, blue hour, sunrise, golden hour and a clear midday blue. Each one follows the sun's real height, so the evening plays the morning back in reverse. After dusk the stars come out and wheel slowly across the sky, rising and setting like the moon. Drag the slider under the watch, press play to watch a whole day, or jump to a moment:

Latitude

40° N

Pick the band you live in. With the date, that's all the face needs to work out sunrise, sunset and how high the sun climbs, so the sky keeps up with the seasons by itself. The sun is amber on the horizon and whitens as it rises, and its glow grows with it. It never asks for your location.

Weather

Partly cloudy

With Show weather on, an icon and the temperature sit above the time. The sky follows the conditions too: grey under cloud and snow, darker in the rain.

Moon phase

Live

At night the moon takes the sun's place on the arc. Live follows the real phase, or you can keep your favourite.

Middle bar

Seconds

The thin line along the horizon fills up. Pick what it counts: seconds, minutes or battery. You can also turn it off. Minutes and battery stay on in always-on mode.

Bottom complication

Single line

Single line puts one long complication under the date, your next event by default, and you can swap in any long-text one. Curved pair swaps it for two mirrored complications that follow the sun's arc up either side, battery and steps to start, each able to show a progress bar, short text or an icon. The curved pair needs Wear OS 7.

And the rest

Tap the date to open your calendar, or the time to open your alarms. Dark ground ends the sky at the horizon and lets its glow fade into black below.

Install

On your wrist in a minute

WatchSky is free on Google Play for watches with Wear OS 5.1 or newer. It runs entirely on the watch.

1

Get it from Google Play

Open the Play Store on your watch and search for WatchSky, or use the Google Play button on this page and install it to your watch.

2

Phone won't offer it?

Some phones don't list watch faces. The same listing then installs a small WatchSky phone app. Tap Install on watch in it and the listing opens on your paired watch.

3

Set it as your face

  1. Long-press your current watch face.
  2. Swipe and tap Add watch face (+).
  3. Pick WatchSky from the list.

New in 1.4

  • Pick your latitude instead of sunrise and sunset times. The sky now follows the real sun, so dawn and dusk shift with the seasons.
  • The sun turns amber near the horizon and whitens as it climbs, and its glow grows with it.

  • A new Dark ground option ends the sky at the horizon with a soft glow.
  • Tap the clock to open your alarms.
  • The Show date and always-on complication toggles now work reliably, and TalkBack reads out the weather.
